# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient and specific. Constructive technical criticism is welcome. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unsolicited promotions and publishing another person's private information are not.

Keep discussions relevant to YOEAI. Never post credentials, private conversations or proprietary files without authorization. Use the private reporting route in SECURITY.md for vulnerabilities.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the address in SECURITY.md. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Reports are considered in context; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is necessary to address harm. There is no guaranteed response time.
