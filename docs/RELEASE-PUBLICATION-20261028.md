This publication makes the verified Normal source snapshot available for developers. It is not a full Windows installer release. No model weights, inference executables, private profiles or optional runtimes are attached.

## Other-PC Lite test

The maintainer reports that Lite installed successfully on another PC and that chat worked with and without internet. These are user-reported passes. Restart persistence, exact target-asset hash and update/uninstall were not independently confirmed. Normal 16GB has not been reported tested on that other PC. This evidence does not qualify every optional voice, creative or agent runtime.

Optional models/runtimes keep their own licenses. No newly qualified complete installer is claimed, and no old setup EXE is substituted.
